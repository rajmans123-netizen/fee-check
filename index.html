How to get the bill
Getting the bill screenshot
  1. Open your food or grocery app → Orders → any past order.
  2. Scroll to Bill summary / Bill details and take a screenshot.
  3. Add one, or a few at once for your total.

Each bill

Honest limits: it reads bill screenshots in English from apps like Swiggy, Zomato, Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart. Fees differ by city, time and cart, so it only counts what's printed on your bill. A blurry or cut-off screenshot can misread a number: if a bill doesn't add up, it says so, and you can tap any amount to fix it. Your screenshots never leave your phone.
